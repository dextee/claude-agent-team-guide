## What would your team like to stop repeating?

An agent team helps when a recurring task needs several kinds of work: reading a request, checking approved information, preparing an action and handling exceptions. Start with the business process and the result you need, not the model.

Examples to explore include invoice checks, prospect research, support routing and routine booking questions. Each needs its own data access, controls and acceptance criteria.

Start with a process description and sanitized examples. Credentials, customer records and confidential files belong in an agreed secure channel after access and handling are defined.

## What happens next

1. **Check the fit.** Confirm the process, decision maker, involvement, budget and timing.
2. **Agree the scope.** Document inputs, integrations, human approvals, exceptions, deliverables and terms.
3. **Build and evaluate.** Test representative cases and failure handling against an agreed baseline.
4. **Release and measure.** Confirm ownership and support; track the result after launch.

A stronger model alone does not make a useful business outcome. The evidence that matters is whether the finished workflow meets its acceptance criteria at an acceptable total cost.
